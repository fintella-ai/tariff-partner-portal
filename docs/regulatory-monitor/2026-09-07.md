## Summary of Changes Found

### 1. 🔴 Section 122 expired July 24, 2026 — replaced by Section 301 Forced Labor tariffs

**Status:** Already expired; successor tariff now active. Seed data updated.

- Section 122's 150-day statutory maximum ran out at 12:01 AM ET July 24, 2026
- The Administration replaced it at the exact same minute with new **Section 301 Forced Labor** tariffs on **60 economies** at **10% or 12.5%** (covers ~99.4% of US imports by value)
  - 12.5% on 46 economies with no prohibition on forced-labor imports
  - 10% on 14 economies with a prohibition but ineffective enforcement (Canada, Ecuador, EU, Indonesia, Mexico, Pakistan + 8 others)
- Legal basis: Trade Act of 1974 Section 301 (19 U.S.C. 2411) — no 150-day cap, no Congressional override mechanism
- USTR investigation was launched March 12, 2026

**Impact on calculator:** These are NOT IEEPA tariffs. CAPE/refund eligibility is unaffected — CAPE processes only duties paid under IEEPA authority (Feb 1, 2025 – Feb 23, 2026). The new Section 301 charges are a separate forward-looking obligation. Seed data updated with `S301-FORCED-LABOR-01` entry to document the successor regime for completeness.

**Source:** [Honigman alert](https://www.honigman.com/alert-3462), [Baker Donelson alert](https://www.bakerdonelson.com/forced-labor-section-301-tariffs-imposed-to-replace-expiring-section-122-tariffs-what-importers-need-to-know), [RVIA](https://www.rvia.org/news-insights/section-122-tariffs-replaced-new-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs)

### 2. 🟡 Section 122 CIT invalidation (May 7, 2026) — stayed pending appeal

**Status:** CIT ruled unlawful; Federal Circuit stayed the ruling; tariffs continued through expiration. Noted in seed data.

- May 7, 2026: CIT (2-1) held Section 122 10% global tariff unlawful — statutory balance-of-payments deficit condition not satisfied (*Burlap & Barrel, Inc. v. United States*)
- Injunction ran only as to named plaintiffs (Burlap & Barrel, Basic Fun, State of Washington)
- May 12: DoJ filed appeal + motion to stay; Federal Circuit issued administrative stay
- June 11: Federal Circuit granted full stay pending appeal — Section 122 collected through Jul 23, 2026 expiration
- Appeal continues; refundability of Section 122 duties paid is TBD

**Impact on calculator:** CAPE does not cover Section 122 duties (those are for the Feb 24 – Jul 23, 2026 period, after IEEPA ended). No calculator logic change needed. Seed data notes updated with litigation status.

**Source:** [Skadden alert](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/05/us-trade-court-strikes-down-section-122-tariffs), [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/tax/u-s-court-of-international-trade-invalidates-section-122-tariffs), [Green Worldwide](https://www.greenworldwide.com/federal-circuit-temporarily-stays-section-122-tariff-ruling-pending-appeal/)

### 3. 🟡 CAPE Phase 2 launched June 29, 2026 — reconciliation-flagged entries now accepted

**Status:** Live since June 29, 2026. Eligibility logic updated.

- CAPE Phase 2 deployed June 29, 2026 per CBP Trade Information Notice (Jul 23, 2026 update confirms active)
- Accepts **Type 01, 02, 06 entries** that are **flagged for reconciliation** (where the Type 09 reconciliation entry has not yet been filed), consistent with same Phase 1 constraints (unliquidated or within 80 days of liquidation)
- The Type 09 reconciliation entry itself remains excluded from CAPE

**Impact on calculator:**
- Added `isFlaggedForReconciliation` field to `EntryForEligibility` interface
- Added `"cape_phase2"` as a new `FilingMethod` type
- `checkEligibility()` now routes Type 01/02/06 entries with `isFlaggedForReconciliation: true` to `"cape_phase2"` filing method before the excluded-type check
- Updated `EXCLUDED_ENTRY_TYPES` comment block to document Phase 2 and Phase 3 context

**Source:** CBP Trade Information Notice (Jul 23, 2026), [CAPE Phase 2 launch date guide](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/cape-phase-2-launch-date-whats-coming-may-2026), [Brady Ware CAPE guide](https://bradyware.com/tariff-refund-faq/)

### 4. 🟡 CAPE Phase 3 temporarily delayed — no new deployment date

**Status:** Deployment held since Aug 25, 2026. Calculator updated with `"cape_phase3"` method type; no eligibility route yet (litigation remains the current path).

- Phase 3 was originally scheduled to deploy August 20, 2026
- CBP notified CIT on August 25, 2026 that Phase 3 is **temporarily delayed** while CBP builds additional system validations to avoid inadvertently modifying non-IEEPA duties during reliquidation of finally-liquidated entries
- As of September 7, 2026: **no new deployment date announced**
- Phase 3 will only apply to importers who have **filed lawsuits at CIT** (not all importers)
- As of August 21, 2026: ~$132.5B in IEEPA refunds accepted for CAPE processing; ~$106.6B completed and sent to Treasury

**Impact on calculator:** Added `"cape_phase3"` to `FilingMethod` type for future use once Phase 3 deploys. No routing change yet — finally-liquidated entries for litigants are not routable to Phase 3 until CBP announces a new date. Documented in code comment.

**Source:** [Green Worldwide Phase 3 update](https://www.greenworldwide.com/cape-phase-3-for-ieepa-tariff-refunds-on-track-for-end-of-july-as-federal-circuit-appeal-continues/), [JM Rodgers CBP update](https://www.jmrodgers.com/cbp-ieepa-refund-update-132-5-billion-accepted-for-cape-processing-as-phase-3-is-delayed/)

### 5. 🟡 Lead IEEPA refund case changed — Freestyle World replaces Euro-Notions Florida

**Status:** Noted; no code/data change required.

- **Euro-Notions Florida, Inc. v. United States (CIT No. 25-00595)** voluntarily dismissed
- CIT Judge Eaton designated **Freestyle World, Inc. v. United States (CIT No. 26-01088)** as the new lead case for consolidated IEEPA refund litigation
- July 17, 2026: Judge Eaton ordered CBP to reliquidate, without IEEPA duties, all plaintiff entries liquidated >80 days — this is the Phase 3 scope (held pending system readiness)
- The parallel V.O.S. Selections, Inc. v. United States (No. 25-00066) case on non-plaintiff refunds continues

**Impact on calculator:** No data or logic change. Relevant for legal context — finally-liquidated importer-plaintiffs in Freestyle World are the beneficiaries of the Phase 3 order; non-plaintiffs remain in a "litigation only" posture until Phase 3 + any universal order for non-plaintiffs.

**Source:** [Thompson Hine SmarTrade](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/07/cit-orders-cbp-to-process-ieepa-tariff-refunds-for-phase-3-finally-liquidated-entries/), [Cherry Bekaert](https://www.cbh.com/insights/alerts/ieepa-tariff-update-refunds-on-final-entries-in-dispute/), [Subject to Inquiry](https://www.subjecttoinquiry.com/2026/06/as-courts-wrestle-with-tariff-refund-cases-importers-should-confirm-submission-of-key-information-and-assess-options/)

Type 47 drawback entries remain excluded from both CAPE Phase 1 and Phase 2. CBP has stated these will be addressed in a future phase. No timeline announced. The `isDrawback` check in `checkEligibility()` correctly routes these to `filingMethod: "none"`.
