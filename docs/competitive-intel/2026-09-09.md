# Competitive Intelligence Report — 2026-09-09

## Summary

Four months after the May 2026 scan, the IEEPA refund calculator market has matured significantly: CBP has processed ~$106.6B of the $132.5B accepted in claims, making the window increasingly defined (Phase 3 for "finally liquidated" entries is delayed). The major competitive development is **Wove's confirmed native TMS integrations** with CargoWise, Magaya, GoFreight, and Logiware — directly eroding Fintella's primary differentiator. Three notable new entrants emerged: **1st Capital Financial** (calculator + advance financing + full-service filing), **NormSuite** (SaaS compliance toolkit), and **Airlift USA** (freight forwarder lead-gen). CustomsGenius is expanding from IEEPA into a broader tariff compliance platform. The government's Federal Circuit appeal creates market uncertainty that none of the self-serve calculators are addressing in product — an opening for Fintella's attorney-network (Frost Law) positioning.

## New Entrants

Three verifiable new entrants were identified since the May 2026 scan:

### NE-11. 1st Capital Financial — https://1stcapitalfinancial.com/ieepa-tariff-refund/
**⚠️ HIGH IMPACT — Calculator + Financing + Full Service**

- **Free IEEPA Tariff Refund Calculator** at `/ieepa-tariff-refund/calculator/`: ES-003 upload, client-side browser processing, IEEPA line identification, refund principal + statutory interest estimate, **CAPE Declaration CSV generation**.
- **IEEPA Tariff Advance**: Cash advance against pending IEEPA refund claims — bridges the 60–90 day CBP processing window. Targets importers who need liquidity now.
- **File and Fund Program™**: Full-service managed filing — licensed customs brokers + trade specialists + legal + tax advisors. Covers Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3 filings. Financing embedded. Vast network of commercial and alternative lenders. No upfront costs.
- **Target market**: Claims $1M–$5M and $5M–$10M; largest serviced: $250M.
- **Positioning**: "We file it, we fund it." Most complete vertically-integrated offering in the market — calculator is free lead-gen for the financing business.
- **⚠️ Assessment**: The "IEEPA Tariff Advance" product is TariffClaim.ai's investor-financing angle evolved into a proper lending product. This is the most direct competition to Fintella's Dossier-to-Deal pipeline because it captures large importers before they need a broker or law firm. The calculator having free CAPE CSV generation also overlaps with Fintella's validation feature.

### NE-12. NormSuite — https://normsuite.com / https://tariff.normsuite.com
**MODERATE IMPACT — SaaS Compliance Suite for SMB**

- **Platform**: Eight regulation compliance SaaS tools for small and mid-size businesses.

- **Assessment**: Not a direct CAPE filing competitor but competes for mindshare and relationship with the SMB importer. If NormSuite adds CAPE CSV generation to their SaaS suite, this becomes a more significant threat.

### NE-13. Airlift USA — https://airliftusa.com/ieepa-calculator

### NE-14. Gateway Lines — https://tariff.gatewaylines.com/ieepa-refund
**⚠️ MODERATE-HIGH IMPACT — AI-Powered CF-7501 Upload**

## Market Context: Critical Developments Since May 2026

### CAPE Progress (as of August 21, 2026)
- **$132.5B** in claims accepted for processing
- **$106.6B** completed and sent to Treasury
- **22,170 refunds ($1.7B)** stuck — missing ACH bank account information in ACE
- The stuck-ACH cohort is an addressable market: importers who filed but haven't received funds because their ACE banking profile is incomplete

### CAPE Phase 3 (Finally Liquidated Entries) — Delayed
- Originally targeted for late July 2026
- As of August 25, 2026: CBP confirmed Phase 3 is **temporarily delayed** to build new validations
- Phase 3 scope is narrower than expected: only applies to importers who filed CIT lawsuits
- CAPE June 29, 2026 update: now accepts reconciliation entries (types 01, 02, 06)

### Government Federal Circuit Appeal
- DOJ filed Notice of Appeal to the Federal Circuit on June 2, 2026
- Oral arguments held August 19, 2026
- **Does not halt refund processing** — Phase 1 and 2 refunds continue
- Creates uncertainty specifically for Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries)
- The government's argument: CIT orders for "universal injunction" relief to importers who did not file CIT protective actions are impermissible
- **Implication for Fintella/Frost Law**: Importers who haven't sued at CIT and are waiting for Phase 3 may need to file protective CIT actions NOW — this is an urgent legal services opportunity for Frost Law

## Recommended Actions

### 1. 🚨 URGENT — Reframe TMS Embedding as "CAPE Pipeline," Not Just Calculator Embed
Wove now has confirmed CargoWise and Magaya integrations with FreightWaves coverage. Fintella can no longer claim "only TMS-embedded calculator" without qualification. **Recommended action**: Reframe Fintella's TMS embed as the **only CAPE filing pipeline inside a TMS** — emphasizing the Dossier-to-Deal workflow (calculate → refer → file → track commission) rather than just "calculator in CargoWise." The multi-client dashboard, per-importer grouping, commission tracking, and Dossier-to-Deal pipeline are genuinely unique. Wove is a tariff intelligence platform; Fintella is a claims management pipeline. Make that distinction explicit in marketing copy and the TMS widget UI.

### 2. ⚠️ HIGH — Add CAPE Error Reference / Recovery Guide
CustomsGenius launched a dedicated CAPE Error Reference at `/cape-errors` — no other competitor has this. With 22,170 refunds ($1.7B) currently stuck due to ACH issues and Phase 3 validations being built, brokers are going to hit CAPE errors in volume. **Recommended action**: Build a CAPE error reference section in Fintella TIE — or at minimum a knowledge article at `/docs/cape-errors` — covering the most common CBP rejection codes, ACH bank account setup steps, and reconciliation entry handling. This is high-leverage content that drives broker stickiness. It pairs naturally with the CAPE CSV pre-validation differentiator: "We validate before you submit so you don't see these errors."

### 3. ⚠️ HIGH — Address the Government Appeal in Product Messaging
The DOJ's Federal Circuit appeal (oral arguments August 19) creates genuine uncertainty for Phase 3 (finally liquidated entries). None of the calculator competitors are addressing this in their product UI. **Recommended action**: Add a Phase 3 status indicator to the Fintella TIE dashboard — showing current Phase 3 status (delayed, reopened, closed), court proceedings context, and a clear CTA: "Talk to Frost Law about filing a protective CIT action if you have finally liquidated entries." This turns the legal uncertainty into a Frost Law referral opportunity and positions Fintella as the most legally-informed tool in the market.

### 4. MEDIUM — Monitor 1st Capital Financial's Financing Angle
1st Capital Financial has the most vertically integrated offering: free calculator + CAPE CSV + managed filing + cash advance + lender network. This targets the same large-importer segment ($1M–$250M claims) that Frost Law services. The "no upfront cost" financing model may be more attractive to importers than paying broker/legal fees upfront. **Recommended action**: Evaluate whether Fintella should add a referral to a financing partner (factoring company or invoice advance lender) for Frost Law clients — framing it as "file with Frost Law, advance 80% of your expected refund within 48 hours." This matches 1st Capital's offer with Fintella's attorney quality.
